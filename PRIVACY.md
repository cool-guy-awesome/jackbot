# Privacy Policy — Jackbot

**Last Updated:** November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Jackbot ("the Bot") handles user information when operating within Discord servers.

## 1. Information We Process
To perform its core function, the Bot requires access to Discord's `Message Content` intent. When active in a server, the Bot temporarily reads the following data in real time:
* **Message Content:** Scanned solely in memory to detect specific trigger words.
* **User IDs:** Checked solely to prevent the Bot from responding to itself or specific excluded user accounts.
* **Channel IDs:** Used solely to send automated replies back to the channel where a trigger word was detected.

## 2. Data Storage and Retention
**The Bot does not store, log, or save any user data.**
* Message contents, User IDs, and Channel IDs are processed strictly in temporary volatile memory (RAM) and are discarded immediately after the message is evaluated.
* No databases, text logs, or external analytics services are used to retain user information.

## 3. Data Sharing
Because the Bot does not collect or store any personal data, **no user data is ever sold, rented, or shared** with third parties.

## 4. Third-Party Services
The Bot operates exclusively on the Discord platform. Your interactions with the Bot are also governed by Discord’s own [Privacy Policy](https://discord.com).

## 5. Children's Privacy
The Bot is intended for use by individuals who meet Discord's minimum age requirements. Since no data is collected or stored, no information from minors is knowingly retained.

## 6. Changes to This Policy
This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in the Bot's functionality. Continued use of the Bot after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
